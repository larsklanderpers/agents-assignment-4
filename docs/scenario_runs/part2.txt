### 2. Common Solutions for Password Reset Issues

When customers report issues resetting passwords, practical troubleshooting follows this sequence:

1. **Verify Account Identifier & Email Accuracy:**
   * Confirm the email address entered on the reset form matches the active account email (`john.doe@example.com`) exactly, without typos or extra whitespace.
2. **Check Spam, Junk, and Corporate Mail Filters:**
   * Automated reset emails often get routed to spam/junk folders or caught by strict domain-level mail filtering rules.
3. **Request a Fresh Reset Link:**
   * Reset links/tokens have short expiration windows and are single-use. If the previous link expired or failed, request a new link and click it promptly upon receipt.
4. **Isolate Browser and Session Factors:**
   * Browser extensions, autofill managers, or cached redirect loops can disrupt reset flows. Test the link in a **private/incognito browser window** or after clearing browser cache and cookies.
5. **Security Practice:**
   * Never request, accept, or handle customer passwords, verification codes, or reset tokens directly.
