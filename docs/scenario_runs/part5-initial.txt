### Common Causes & Recommended Solutions

Password reset failures typically stem from one of the following areas:

1. **Email Delivery & Filtering**
   * **Issue:** Reset emails may be delayed, filtered into spam/junk, or blocked by corporate mail security.
   * **Solution:** Check spam/junk folders, verify the exact spelling of the registered account email, and ensure the sending domain is added to safe senders.

2. **Link Expiration & Validity**
   * **Issue:** Reset links expire after a short window (typically 15–60 minutes) or become invalid if multiple reset requests are triggered in quick succession.
   * **Solution:** Ensure the link opened is from the most recent email received, or request a fresh link if the time limit has passed.

3. **Browser & Cache Conflicts**
   * **Issue:** Cached credentials, autofill, browser extensions, or email link-wrapping can corrupt reset tokens.
   * **Solution:** Open the reset link in a private/incognito browsing window, copy and paste the URL directly into the address bar, or clear browser cookies and cache.

4. **Account Status & Lockouts**
   * **Issue:** Accounts temporarily locked due to repeated failed login attempts or marked inactive may prevent self-service resets.
   * **Solution:** Verify that the account status is active. If locked, an administrator must intervene to unlock the account.

5. **Administrative Override**
   * **Solution:** If self-service resets continue to fail, an administrator can trigger a manual password reset email or provide a temporary one-time password/verification link. *(Note: Support representatives should never request passwords or reset tokens directly from customers).*
